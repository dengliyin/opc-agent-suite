# Video Assembly Agent Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Clean commercial short-video assembly for product selling clips. The source videos remain the hero; overlays must feel useful and restrained, with captions readable on busy footage and no decorative UI competing with the product or face.

## Colors

- Canvas: #f7f3ee
- Caption text: #fffaf2
- Caption outline: #15110d
- Caption plate: rgba(18, 15, 12, 0.58)
- Accent: #d8b56d

## Typography

- Captions: Avenir Next, Arial, sans-serif
- Metadata: Charter, Georgia, serif

## Motion

- Gentle caption lift and opacity entrance.
- Short warm wash at segment boundaries.
- No decorative text overlays, large title cards, or decorative cards.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not cover faces or products with large text.
- Do not add marketing badges, carts, arrows, or fake platform UI.
- Do not recolor or crop the original product video aggressively.
- Do not use dark/purple gradient backgrounds.
